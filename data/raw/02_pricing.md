# Nimbus Cloud — Pricing

Nimbus Cloud pricing is based on the number of rows synced per month,
with separate tiers for connector access and support level.

## Plans

**Starter — $99/month**
- Up to 5 million rows synced per month
- Access to 30 core connectors
- Daily sync frequency only
- Community support (forum + email, 48-hour response target)

**Growth — $499/month**
- Up to 50 million rows synced per month
- Access to all 120+ connectors
- Hourly sync frequency
- Email support with a 24-hour response target
- One free onboarding call included

**Enterprise — custom pricing**
- Unlimited rows, negotiated per contract
- Streaming/CDC sync mode included
- Dedicated Slack channel with a named solutions engineer
- 99.9% uptime SLA with financial penalties for breaches
- Self-hosted deployment option available
- Custom connector development available at an additional fee

## Overage Charges

Starter and Growth plans that exceed their monthly row limit are charged
$8 per additional million rows, billed at the end of the billing cycle.
Enterprise plans do not have overage charges since row volume is
negotiated directly into the contract.

## Free Trial

New accounts receive a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan, including
full connector access, before being downgraded to Starter or asked to
choose a paid plan. No credit card is required to start a trial.

## Annual Billing Discount

Customers who commit to annual billing receive a 15% discount on
Starter and Growth plans. Enterprise contracts are always negotiated
individually and are not eligible for this standard discount.
